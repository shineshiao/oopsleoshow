Privacy Policy: Oops Leo Publisher

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Oops Leo Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Oops, Leo! channel (@oopsleoshow) to post the channel's own videos to its own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, it uses the Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel that the owner connects. It reads their IDs and names, posts videos and captions to them, adds a comment to its own posts, and deletes its own test uploads.

YouTube. The tool uses the YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What it stores. It stores a Facebook Page access token and a YouTube sign-in in the owner's computer's secure keychain, plus the IDs and links of the posts it makes. It doesn't collect, store or share any data about other people, such as viewers, followers or commenters.

Sharing. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting data. See the Data Deletion Instructions page. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in Facebook settings, or in Google account settings for YouTube.
