Meet us

Leo and Lily

Leo

The confused tourist

Leo is new in town and tries his best in English. He mixes up words everyone mixes up, so you don't have to.

Lily

The sarcastic local

Lily has heard it all before. She rolls her eyes, then explains the rule in plain words with an example you'll remember.

How it works

Every video in three steps

  1. Oops!

    Leo uses the wrong word in a real situation, like asking for the wrong street.

  2. The rule

    Lily explains the difference. Every meaning is checked against the Cambridge and Oxford Learner's Dictionaries.

  3. Your turn

    A quick quiz ends the video. Write your answer in the comments, and we reply with the right one later.
