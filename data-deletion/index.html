Data Deletion Instructions: Oops Leo Publisher

Oops Leo Publisher stores no data about anyone except a Facebook Page access token, a YouTube sign-in and post IDs on the channel owner's own computer.

To remove the app's access to your Facebook or Instagram account:

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find Oops Leo Publisher and choose Remove.

To remove its access to your YouTube channel:

  1. Go to your Google account's third-party connections.
  2. Find Oops Leo Publisher and remove its access.

Either way, access ends at once. To ask for anything else to be deleted, write to oops.leo.show@gmail.com, and we will confirm deletion within 30 days.
